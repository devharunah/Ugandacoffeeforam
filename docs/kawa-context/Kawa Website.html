FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Reach our team on Instagram.

What is Kawa?
Kawa is a Ugandan company building digital tools for coffee farmers and agriculture. Our flagship app, KawaScan, diagnoses plant disease from a photo; we also run a marketplace and an agronomy knowledge guide.
Are you planning to support iPhone?
Android comes first because it's what most farmers use. An iOS version is on the roadmap — join the list and we'll let you know when it's ready.
How much does it cost to use Kawa?
Getting started is free. KawaScan gives you free scans to begin, then you can buy scan packs with Mobile Money — from UGX 2,000 for 10 scans. The marketplace is free to post on.
What happens after the free testing period?
Nothing is locked away. Once your free scans are used, you simply top up with a scan pack whenever you need one — there's no subscription and no auto-charge.
Is Kawa only available in Uganda?
We're built and tested in Uganda first, but the tools work anywhere coffee grows. We're expanding across East Africa — tell us where you farm and we'll prioritise it.
